What is different
Parser: the shared parser reads numeric dates month-first even when the tenant's date convention is day-first. In R-41203, an app booking for 2 December appears in the invite as 12 February.
Form hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY, but for a day-first tenant its form interprets the entry day-first. In R-41259, a form booking for 11 April appears as 4 November. The visible instruction and interpretation must agree; this is not evidence that the shared parser alone caused this report.

These mechanisms come from the supplied KB logic and tenant data. The production parser and form were not supplied; reproductions against the actual product remain to be performed. What the desk should do next

Match a report to a demonstrated mechanism, not just a similar subject, browserLocale, or intakeTag. App/email reports and form reports can differ even for the same tenant.
Track the shared-parser and booking-v3 hint problems in separate issues. Keep the original report IDs and the snapshot date with each count.
